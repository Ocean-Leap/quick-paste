# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
the quick-paste project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: using
welcoming and inclusive language, respecting differing viewpoints, gracefully
accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct
that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers through
GitHub (a private security-advisory report or a direct message). Maintainers
will review and respond, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions and
comments that violate this code, or ban temporarily or permanently any
contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate.
